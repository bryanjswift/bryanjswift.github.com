Experience

  1. Lead Software Engineer at Little Cinema Digital from May 2021

    Little Cinema Digital is a digital agency that partners with brands to produce live digital events built around interactivity and high quality video. Its clients include Netflix, Disney, and Amazon. LCD has twice been chosen to produce Netflix's Upfronts, where Netflix previews its upcoming slate to win over advertisers, and a critically important event on Netflix's business calendar.

    I joined to support client projects and went on to architect and build the platform LCD now uses to create and run every site it produces. When I arrived, each new client meant a new site cloned from a rigid template system. Those sites were unreliable, struggled under the traffic spikes that come with successful events, and the business had to turn away clients whose ideas didn't fit the templates. I helped design and build a flexible content management system tailored to LCD's business to enable the team to assemble reliable sites with custom behavior without opening a code editor. Every event now runs on proven, reusable components, which let LCD serve its existing clients with confidence and expand into new markets. Each guest site is served in isolation, so one event's traffic can never affect another's, and the platform has handled events with as many as 150,000 concurrent guests, against a typical event of around 1,000.

    An event site's biggest moment is when a marketing push sends an audience to it, and those were exactly the sites where we saw high bounce rates. We traced much of the problem to initial load times of three to five seconds, well above industry norms. I reworked how our sites were delivered, cut the initial JavaScript payload and reduced the work done in the browser from that initial payload, bringing initial load times under one second.

    The platform is a Remix front end and a TypeScript GraphQL API running on AWS Lambda, with Rust for the most performance sensitive paths. Data lives in PostgreSQL and DynamoDB, and video is processed and streamed through AWS MediaConvert and MediaPackage. Infrastructure is defined with CDK. Every commit to main deploys to production through GitHub Actions, and AWS X-Ray with custom spans shows us what is happening after deploy.

    Beyond the code, I established development practices that brought a level of rigor and discipline to software engineering at LCD that hadn't existed before. I introduced TypeScript and taught the team to design systems around types, which let us build faster with fewer defects. I also introduced Architecture Decision Records, so every major platform change has a record of what we chose, why, and which alternatives we considered and rejected. ADRs moved the team from jumping straight into implementation to researching and discussing changes first.

    The engineering organization has ranged from fifteen people at its peak to three today. As part of my responsibilities with the larger team, I onboarded new engineers and mentored and coached the more junior of them. Throughout, I have kept the CEO and CTO informed on where projects stand and what needs to change, and worked closely with account, design, and executive stakeholders to turn client ambitions into plans we can ship.
